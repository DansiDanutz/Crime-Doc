# CHANNEL DNA — LOCKED SOURCE OF TRUTH

> Style profile extracted from the Fern / Hoog "armchair documentary" register.
> This file is **canonical**. Every script, prompt, and thumbnail in this repo must
> conform to it. Do not edit casually — changing this changes the whole channel.

---

## 1. Naming pattern
- 1–2 words, short, punchy, abstract (not topical).
- Lowercase / stylized, often a trailing period (`fern.`, `umbra.`).
- Optional suffix: `TV` / `Files` / `Documentaries`.
- Handle format: `@name-tv` / `@name-youtube`.

## 2. Description register
- Conversational, lowercase, slightly ironic, self-deprecating.
- 3–10 words. No buzzwords, no exclamation marks. Often hints at cadence ("almost weekly").

## 3. Logo / banner identity
- Black-and-white minimalist; **one** optional accent (green or red).
- Simple geometric marks: circle, dot, single stylized letter.
- Generous negative space / headroom. No gradients, no photos, no maximalism.

## 4. Writing DNA (the core)

**Cold open** (first 3–4 sentences, ~30–40 words):
- Exact date + exact location + one concrete physical action.
- Does **not** explain the topic yet — drops the viewer inside a moment.
- Followed by ONE anchor line stating the subject ("this is the story of…", "this is how he was caught.").

**Sentence rhythm:**
- Short declaratives chained, 5–12 words sweet spot.
- Present tense for the unfolding narrative; past tense for context/background.
- Occasional ultra-short impact line ("It works." / "No answer." / "He blacks out.").
- One longer expository sentence per 4–6 short ones.
- Adjectives reserved for physical descriptors and hard numbers, not emotion.
- Specifics over abstraction: distances (`400 ft or 120 m`), money (`$23 million`), exact times (`9:41 am`), counts (`13 people, more than 6,000 injured`).

**Tone:** deadpan, clinical, observational — calm even over horror. Direct viewer address only at structural pivots. Mild dry irony on absurd facts; never sarcasm, never moralizing. One striking line per ~200 words.

**Structural moves:** cold open → context expansion → mechanism (step-by-step) → implication/aftermath. Rhetorical questions as act pivots. Lists of three. The tangential detail that turns out to be load-bearing. Heavy procedural reconstruction with precise timing.

**Cliffhanger / closing** (final 2–4 sentences, last line ≤12 words) — pick ONE:
1. Unresolved present — the threat never ended.
2. Implicating turn — pivot to viewer/society.
3. Next-mystery tease.
4. Unanswered "why" / "we don't know."
5. Lingering image — return to the cold-open image, recharged.

Final line lands on a noun, name, date, or flat declarative. **Never a clean resolution.**

**NEVER:** "In this video…", "Today we're going to…", "Have you ever wondered…", clickbait ("INSANE", "shocking truth"), emotional editorializing ("tragically", "horrifically"), clean resolved endings.

## 5. Pacing
- **2.5 words/second** (range 2.4–2.7).
- `word_count = target_minutes × 60 × 2.5`. (5 min ≈ 750 words; 8 min ≈ 1,200.)

## 6. Thumbnail DNA
- Palette: bold **red**, pure **white**, deep **black**.
- Focal subject = solid matte RED figure, isolated/singled out.
- Antagonists = solid BLACK (FBI/SWAT) or photoreal uniforms, flanking/aiming at the red figure.
- Background crowds = glossy white mannequins (isolation).
- Text: short ALL-CAPS sans tags in rectangular boxes with a small **triangular pointer** at the relevant head. Red tag = subject ("SPY", "MOLE", "IQ 160"); black tag = institution ("FBI", "CARTEL"). 1–3 words.
- Composition: medium/medium-wide; confrontation/capture/identification moment.
- Emotion: isolation, exposure, paranoia, being watched, "this person is the one."
- Lighting: cinematic key from above, soft contact shadows, volumetric atmosphere.

## 7. Image-generation template (verbatim structure)
```
Cinematic photorealistic 3D render, [NUMBER] glossy white featureless mannequin
figures with smooth eggshell heads (no facial features), wearing detailed
[UNIFORM TYPE] with [uniform details], [ACTION/POSE], in a [ENVIRONMENT with
specific props], [LIGHTING], shallow depth of field, volumetric light, dust
particles in air, cinematic color grade, Unreal Engine 5, octane render, 8K,
anamorphic lens, shot on ARRI Alexa, [ASPECT RATIO]
```

## 8. Mannequin-world color code (LOCKED)
| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Glossy **white** mannequin | civilian / crowd / bystander / neutral |
| Solid matte **RED** mannequin | protagonist / focal subject |
| Solid matte **BLACK** mannequin | institutional antagonist (FBI, police, military) |
| Photoreal clothing over white body | role where the uniform carries information |

- Eggshell featureless heads: no eyes/mouth/nose/ears/hair.
- Realistic human proportions, polished plastic-white surface.
- Environments: **Mode A** (infinite white void, contact shadows only) or **Mode B** (sparse photoreal location with mannequins inserted).
- Render language: Unreal Engine 5, octane render, 8K, anamorphic lens, ARRI Alexa, shallow DoF, volumetric light, dust particles, cinematic color grade.
- Tone: clinical, observational, never gory, never horror-lit. Violence implied by composition only.

## 9. Sponsors — PERMANENTLY EXCLUDED

advertiser read are **never** modeled, never reproduced, never written. No "thanks

## 10. Topic territory
Assassinations & attacks, dark-web & hacking, intelligence, cults, aviation/disasters,
justice systems, engineering/history, infrastructure failures, geopolitics —
crime, intelligence, and systems-gone-wrong.
