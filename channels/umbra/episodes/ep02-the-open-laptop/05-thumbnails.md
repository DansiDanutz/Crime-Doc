# STATE 6 — Thumbnail prompts (5 variants)

> Red Ulbricht, black FBI/institution, white patrons. Stark contrast, label tags + triangular
> pointers, minimal bold text. Emotion: exposure / the caught moment. Generate in Nano Banana
> Pro, `count: 4` each.

## Variant 1 — "The Open Laptop"
- **Concept:** red mannequin at a library table, black plainclothes hands lifting the glowing open laptop from behind him as he looks away.
- **Text overlay:** RED tag "CAUGHT" (at red head) · BLACK tag "LOGGED IN" (at the screen)
- **Emotion trigger:** the exact instant of exposure; the thing being taken before he can turn.
- **Composition note:** red figure lower-left looking off-frame right, black hands + glowing laptop upper-right, cold blue screen light against warm library.
- **Prompt:**
```
Cinematic photorealistic 3D render thumbnail, a matte red featureless mannequin (eggshell head,
red hoodie) seated at a public-library table looking away to the right, while two matte black
featureless mannequin hands reach in from behind and lift an open laptop whose screen glows cold
blue, sparse Mode B library, stark high-contrast lighting, key from above, deep shadows, bold red
/ pure white / deep black palette, shallow depth of field, volumetric light, cinematic color
grade, Unreal Engine 5, octane render, 8K, anamorphic lens, shot on ARRI Alexa, 16:9. Overlaid
graphic tags: a RED rectangular tag reading "CAUGHT" with a small triangular pointer at the red
mannequin's head; a BLACK rectangular tag reading "LOGGED IN" with a triangular pointer at the
glowing laptop screen. Bold ALL-CAPS sans-serif, white text.
```
